# Answer

**Use when** you can answer from what you know or can read in a few minutes: the vault, a task record, a
repository, a session's screen. When the answer needs digging in code or systems, use Investigate; when it needs
outside sources, use Research.

**Steps**
1. Laima reads what is needed and answers, saying what she looked at. Bulky reading goes to a subagent inside
   her own harness, whose answer she keeps: her context stays small and no session is launched.

**Ask the user:** nothing.

**Done when** the user has the answer. No record and no log line, unless the answer produced a decision or a follow-up
worth keeping: then open a record (`project: none` when the question concerns no project), write it there, and close it
with outcome `answered`.
