# Investigate

**Use when** the user wants to know what is happening in their code, systems or logs, and why, and finding out
takes real digging. Nothing is changed. A question about the outside world is Research.

**Steps**
1. **Investigator** (`roles/investigator.md`): launch with the question, what is known, and where to look.
2. **Laima** checks the report: do the findings carry evidence, and do the one or two that matter most hold when
   you look yourself? A count the user may pass on to other people (in a ticket comment, a reply) is recounted from
   the rows it is made of, by you or by the investigator, before the user has it: the user acts on the figure and
   may quote it, so a correction after they have it costs a retraction.
3. **Laima** delivers the findings to the user, with the investigator's recommendation and her own.

**Ask the user** when a decision comes out of it: fix now (open a Build task with `source` pointing here), later
(a follow-up in the record), or leave.

**Done when** the user has the findings, and each follow-up is either a new task or written in the record.
Outcome `answered`.
